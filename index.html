At the booth

The main points from the booth instructions, for while you are on shift.

Your role

Be friendly and patient. Help people feel comfortable donating online.

No one should feel pressured to donate, or to donate online.

What you help with

  1. Help people donate online on their own phone.
  2. Answer questions about event donations using the Event Donations sheet (A-170o) or donate.jw.org.
  3. Hand out a printed copy of that sheet when someone asks for one.
  4. Asked “how much should I give?” The amount is a personal decision, and every donation is appreciated. (Luke 21:1-4; 2 Cor. 9:7)
  5. For general questions about ways to donate, show them where the answer is on donate.jw.org.
  6. Want help later? They can ask a trusted brother or sister in their congregation.
  7. Detailed question you cannot answer? Show them Contact Us on donate.jw.org. They type the question in the Comments box and the branch replies.

Never do these

  • No cash or checks at the booth. Those go in a contribution box.
  • Do not donate for someone using your phone or your jw.org account.
  • Do not look at their card or any personal details.
  • Do not see, create or type their password.

If a reporter comes up

Do not give an interview or comments. Send them to the assembly overseer.

Before your shift

  • donate.jw.org: walk through a one-time donation and a recurring one. Know the payment options, Online Help, the FAQ, and Contact Us.
  • jw.org account: know “Create New Account” on the Log In page, and “Trouble Logging In?” for people unsure if they have an account.
  • Event Donations sheet (A-170o): read the answers so you can find them fast.

Tips

  • Let them hold their phone. You point, they tap.
  • Step back or look away when they type a password or card number.
  • Keep printed Event Donations sheets within reach.
